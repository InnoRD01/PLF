About PLF

Formalised in 2021 as a Sikkim-focused, locally-led, non-profit organisation.

🔭

Vision

To build, support and sustain professional learning communities and their individuals to become reflective practitioners, collaborative problem-solvers, and value creators.

🧭

Mission

To be a highly sought-after Learning Service Provider.

🎯

Aim

To catalyse and support the self-directed learning journeys of individuals and organisations through well-facilitated capacity-building programs based on international standards but designed for the local context. They are hyper-relevant, engaging and fun, and run as both online and in-person professional development workshops.

Our Approach

We start with the people doing the work. Programs are built from the bottom up, around what teachers and individuals actually need, and carried forward through continuous professional development.

  • Bottom-up: teacher/individual focused
  • Need analysis
  • Facilitated conversations
  • Continuous professional development
  • Digital literacy
  • Reading
  • Sociocratic governance & self-organisation
  • Reflective practices (debriefs)

Work With Us

Roles at PLF

Learning Consultant

Purpose: Designing and delivering joyful, meaningful, engaging learning solutions.

Accountabilities

  1. Meeting with clients to assess learning needs
  2. Designing customised learning programs aligned with client objectives
  3. Developing well-structured learning programs
  4. Facilitating dynamic and interactive learning sessions to maximise engagement
  5. Evaluating the effectiveness of learning programs through feedback and metrics
  6. Adapting content and delivery methods based on learner and client feedback
  7. Building strong relationships with clients to ensure long-term learning success
  8. Networking to build clientele
  9. Codifying and organising learning programs and processes
